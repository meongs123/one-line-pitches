# Candidate lines — A Day of One-Line Pitches

Field check (research note, 2026-11-24, 3,000 titles + 147 artifact texts):
the field's modal formula is describe-in-plain-words + no-code + working
first version tonight + nothing to lose (verbalize-it framing 82% of
artifacts, plain words 55%, no code 57%). All candidates below are 100%
original, written for this entry, one sentence each, and name make.fun, the
AI agent building, the first version, and a low-risk try, in plain words,
with no invented speeds, prices or guarantees.

## Candidate 1 — Problem-first (165 characters) ✅ FINAL (V2)

> That little thing that bugs you every day is your first app idea: let an AI agent on make.fun build a first version, and try it free to see if the annoyance is gone.

V2 is the final sentence. It supersedes the original 153-character primary
per Red Team revision (F1/F2/F4/F5): V2 names the object explicitly ("your
first app idea"), removes the ambiguous "first version of the fix", replaces
the fragment colon opening with a complete clause, and scopes the object.
The verified-virgin "bugs you" opening and the daily-pain urgency are kept.
The original 153-character wording was:

> That little thing that bugs you every day: let an AI agent on make.fun build a first version of the fix, and try it free to see if the annoyance is gone.

- Fresh angle: the small daily irritation the friend already complains
  about — wording around annoy/frustrat/bugs-you is 0/3,000 titles and
  0/147 artifacts. The whole field pitches the solution; nobody opens on
  the irritation the app would remove.
- Truth: "try it free to see if" promises a try, not a guaranteed fix.

## Candidate 2 — Dialogue, not monologue (142 characters)

> Your idea is only half-formed, so an AI agent on make.fun asks you questions, builds the first version from your answers, and you try it free.

- Fresh angle: the agent asks the friend questions back and forth —
  "asks you" is 0/3,000 titles and 0/147 artifacts. Every entry frames it
  as the friend describing once; none frames it as a conversation.
- Weakness: "half-formed" is a slightly colder open than the irritation
  hook; the question-and-answer turn lands mid-sentence rather than first.

## Candidate 3 — Idea-safety (132 characters)

> Keep your idea yours: an AI agent on make.fun builds your first version without taking it, and you try it free while it stays yours.

- Fresh angle: the idea stays theirs while it is built — "steal" is
  0/3,000 titles and 0/147 artifacts; the theft/safety reassurance itself
  is absent from the field.
- Weakness: "yours" repeats in the same sentence, and the safety promise
  is less concrete than the daily-annoyance hook.

## Candidate 4 — Everyday moment (131 characters)

> Next time the idea hits you on a walk, tell it to an AI agent on make.fun and it builds the first version you can try free tonight.

- Fresh angle: the idea arriving outside desk-time — while driving /
  walking / cooking is 0/3,000 titles and 0/147 artifacts.
- Weakness: shortest of the four and the scene does more work than the
  reason to say yes; "tonight" is the field's most-used speed word (36
  artifacts), so the ending leans on a flooded beat.
